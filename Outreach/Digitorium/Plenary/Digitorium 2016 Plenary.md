# Digitorium 2016 Plenary

We are fortunate in welcoming Professor Thomas H. Luxon of Dartmouth College as our plenary speaker for Digitorium 2016.

Professor Luxon will be presenting his pioneering work in founding The John Milton Reading Room, a major set of digital literary editions, created through scholarly collaboration. This session engages our main focus on digital methods and the communities which these can forge, as well as subject-specific interests in Early Modern studies.

Plenary Title: "A New Media Renaissance: The Case for New Editions of Classic Texts"

Plenary Biography:

Thomas Luxon is Professor of English at Dartmouth College and for nine years was the Cheheyl Professor and founding Director of The Dartmouth Center for the Advancement of Learning which promotes faculty development as teachers. He is the author of two books, Literal Figures: Puritan Allegory and the Reformation Crisis in Representation, and Single Imperfection: Milton Marriage and Friendship. He has published many articles in journals and collections on Bunyan, Shakespeare, Milton and 17th-century religion. He is the General Editor of The John Milton Reading Room, and an eager advocate of using digital technology to improve teaching learning and scholarship. His current book project is called Heroic Restorations; it studies the various attempts, by Dryden, Milton and others, to re-define human heroism in an age characterized by cynicism and satire.
