# Digitorium 2017 Plenary

It is our pleasure and privilege to host 3 plenary sessions at Digitorium 2017, including 2 formal presentations and our first live plenary brainstorming session on virtual reality!

* Scott Gwara (Univ. of South Carolina), creator of MS-Link, will share his project, a database reunifying scattered manuscripts into codices

* Anne MacNeil (Univ. of North Carolina at Chapel Hill) and Deanna Shemek (Univ. of California at Santa Cruz), co-Principal Investigators for interactive digital database of early modern music, manuscripts, and ceramics, the Isabella D'Este Archive, will share their work, and include us in a detailed brainstorming session discussing features that would be good to include in the new virtual reality component of their site, the Virtual Studiolo.
